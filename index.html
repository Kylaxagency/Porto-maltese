Thirty Years of Craft

Rooted in the
Adriatic Shore

Porto Maltese opened its doors in 1993 on Terazije, Belgrade's most storied square. For over three decades we have run a kitchen with a clear point of view: the sea, served fresh, honest, and precise.

Our recipes follow the Adriatic and Tyrrhenian coast — octopus slow-cooked in white wine, pasta dark with cuttlefish ink, carpaccio that arrives cold and translucent. We do not reinvent. We preserve.

At Your Service

How We Welcome You

Fine Dining
White tablecloths, attentive service, and a room that makes the occasion feel right — whether it's a Tuesday or an anniversary.
Seafood Specialties
Every dish begins at the source. Fresh fish and seafood arrive daily and go straight to the kitchen — nothing sits.
Wine List
A considered selection of Mediterranean, Adriatic, and local wines — chosen to complement the food, not compete with it.
Terrace Dining
Terrace seating on Terazije — the best vantage point in Belgrade, with the sea on your plate and the city at your side.
Private Events
We arrange private dinners, corporate lunches, and celebrations with the same care we give every other table.
Reservations
By phone, email, or WhatsApp. Walk-ins are always welcome when the table is free — just call first to be certain.
Free Wi-Fi
High-speed Wi-Fi throughout the restaurant and terrace. For those who find it impossible to truly disconnect.
Pet Friendly
Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the terrace. We will bring a bowl of water without being asked.
